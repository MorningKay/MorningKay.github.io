Iceberg is a project exploring empathetic dialogue generation with large language models. It focuses on modeling deeper emotional states and hidden user needs through an iceberg-style framework.

In this project, I worked on user-agent simulation, dialogue-stage modeling, reward design, and training pipelines for more emotionally aware dialogue agents.
